# Commercial MSME Lending Framework & Regulatory Standards
**Document Identifier**: GOI-MSME-2024-V1 | Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises
**Applicability**: All Scheduled Commercial Banks (SCBs), Small Finance Banks, and NBFCs.

## 1. Statutory MSME Classification Criteria
Under the revised Composite Criteria guidelines (Notification S.O. 2119(E)):
1. **Micro Enterprise**: Investment in Plant & Machinery or Equipment does not exceed ₹1 Crore AND Annual Turnover does not exceed ₹5 Crore.
2. **Small Enterprise**: Investment in Plant & Machinery or Equipment does not exceed ₹10 Crore AND Annual Turnover does not exceed ₹50 Crore.
3. **Medium Enterprise**: Investment in Plant & Machinery or Equipment does not exceed ₹50 Crore AND Annual Turnover does not exceed ₹250 Crore.

## 2. Mandatory Priority Sector Lending (PSL) Targets
1. Domestic scheduled commercial banks are mandated to allocate **40% of Adjusted Net Bank Credit (ANBC)** or Credit Equivalent of Off-Balance Sheet Exposure (CEOBE) to Priority Sector Lending.
2. Within commercial MSME lending, a sub-target of **7.5% of ANBC** is earmarked specifically for Micro Enterprises.

## 3. Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE)
1. **Collateral-Free Limit**: Credit facilities up to **₹500 Lakhs (₹5 Crore)** per eligible borrower entity can be sanctioned without any collateral security or third-party guarantee.
2. **Guarantee Cover**:
   - Up to 85% for Micro enterprises for credit up to ₹5 Lakhs.
   - Up to 75% for default commercial loans up to ₹500 Lakhs.
   - Up to 85% for Women Entrepreneurs, SC/ST borrowers, and units located in NER (North Eastern Region).
3. **Annual Guarantee Fee (AGF)**: Structure ranges from 0.37% to 1.35% p.a. based on loan slab and risk score.

## 4. Universal Ineligible Activities & Prohibitions
The following activities are strictly prohibited from receiving commercial MSME banking facilities:
- Speculative intraday share trading or cryptocurrency purchases (Bitcoin, Ethereum).
- Gambling, casinos, lotteries, and betting operations.
- Real estate speculation or land-banking with no construction activity.
- Personal luxury consumption or vacation financing.
