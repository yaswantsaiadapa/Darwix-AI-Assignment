# MSME Commercial Loan Application Guidelines & Document Checklist
**Document Reference**: MSME-APP-CKLIST-2026
**Compliance Level**: Priority 1 Standard Operating Procedure

## 1. Mandatory Application Documents
To apply for a commercial MSME loan, the applicant enterprise must furnish the following:
1. Udyam MSME Registration Certificate.
2. Business PAN Card and Certificate of Incorporation / Partnership Deed / GST Registration.
3. Last 3 years Audited Financial Statements including Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss statement.
4. Last 12 months comprehensive Current Account Bank Statements.
5. Income Tax Returns (ITR-4 / ITR-5 / ITR-6) for the preceding 2 assessment years.

## 3. Disqualification & Default Criteria
An applicant enterprise shall be summarily disqualified from loan sanctioning under the following conditions:
1. Any director, partner, or proprietor appearing on the RBI Defaulters List or Wilful Defaulters circular.
2. Credit bureau score (CIBIL Commercial / CMR) below the minimum qualifying benchmark of 650.
3. Operating in restricted categories including speculative cryptocurrency mining, lottery distribution, or unregulated chit funds.
