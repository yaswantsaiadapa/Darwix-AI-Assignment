Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE)

The Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME) and Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI) established CGTMSE to facilitate collateral-free credit delivery to micro and small enterprises.

1. Eligible Lending Institutions (MLIs)

Scheduled Commercial Banks (Public, Private, Foreign), Regional Rural Banks, Small Finance Banks, and select Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) accredited as Member Lending Institutions (MLIs) can disburse credit covered by CGTMSE.

2. Scope of Credit Coverage & Loan Types

Both Term Loans and Working Capital facilities (Fund-based and Non-Fund based such as Bank Guarantees and Letters of Credit) up to ₹500 Lakhs (₹5 Crore) are eligible for coverage without third-party guarantee or primary tangible asset collateral.

3. Scheme Coverage Details & Guarantee Percentages

Borrower Category Maximum Credit Cap Guarantee Coverage (%) Primary Collateral
Micro Enterprises (Up to ₹5 Lakhs) ₹5 Lakhs 85% Nil (Collateral-Free)
Women Entrepreneurs / SC / ST Enterprises ₹500 Lakhs 85% Nil (Collateral-Free)
Units in North Eastern Region (NER) / UT of J&K / Ladakh ₹500 Lakhs 85% Nil (Collateral-Free)
All Other Micro & Small Enterprises ₹500 Lakhs 75% Nil (Collateral-Free)

4. Annual Guarantee Fee (AGF) Structure

The annual guarantee fee is calculated on the outstanding loan balance as of 31st March every financial year:

  • Credit up to ₹10 Lakhs: 0.37% p.a.
  • Credit ₹10 Lakhs to ₹50 Lakhs: 0.55% to 0.75% p.a.
  • Credit ₹50 Lakhs to ₹200 Lakhs: 0.60% to 1.20% p.a.
  • Credit ₹200 Lakhs to ₹500 Lakhs: 0.75% to 1.35% p.a.
